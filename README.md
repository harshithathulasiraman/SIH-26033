## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Farm Connect is an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace designed to connect farmers, Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), consumers, retailers and bulk buyers through a single online platform.

The platform eliminates unnecessary intermediaries by allowing farmers to list their agricultural products directly for sale. Consumers and businesses can browse available products, compare prices and purchase fresh agricultural produce directly from verified farmers.

The major features include:

Direct Farmer-to-Consumer Marketplace: Farmers can register and list their agricultural products with quantity, price, location and availability.

FPO Integration: Farmer Producer Organisations can aggregate products from multiple farmers and sell them in bulk.

Bulk Buyer Connection: Hotels, supermarkets, restaurants, wholesalers and retailers can purchase agricultural products directly from farmers.

AI-Based Demand Forecasting: Artificial Intelligence analyses historical sales, seasonal trends and market demand to predict future product requirements.

Smart Logistics Management: The platform connects farmers with delivery partners and transport providers.

Route Optimization: AI-based route planning identifies efficient delivery routes to reduce transportation costs and delivery time.

Transparent Pricing: Farmers and buyers can view prices and negotiate or accept offers without unnecessary intermediaries.

Order and Payment Management: Digital order tracking, payment processing and transaction history provide transparency.

Multilingual Support: Regional language support makes the platform accessible to farmers in rural areas.

Our main objective is to ensure better earnings for farmers, affordable prices for consumers and an efficient agricultural supply chain.

## Feasibility and Viability
Farm Connect is technically and economically feasible because it uses existing web technologies, cloud infrastructure, digital payment systems and mapping services.

Technical Feasibility

Open-source technologies such as Python, React and PostgreSQL can be used.

Machine learning libraries provide tools for demand forecasting.

Existing mapping APIs can support route optimization.

Cloud infrastructure allows the platform to scale as the number of users increases.

Economic Feasibility

The platform can operate with relatively low initial infrastructure costs.

Farmers can register and list products at minimal cost.

Revenue can be generated through optional logistics services, institutional subscriptions and small transaction-based service fees.

Direct transactions can reduce unnecessary supply-chain expenses.

Potential Challenges

Limited digital literacy among rural farmers.

Unpredictable agricultural production and demand.

Transportation and cold-storage limitations.

Maintaining product quality and freshness.

Trust and payment security.

Availability of accurate historical sales data for AI forecasting.

Strategies to Overcome Challenges

Provide a simple interface with regional language support.

Allow FPOs and local coordinators to assist farmers.

Integrate multiple transportation providers.

Introduce farmer and buyer verification.

Implement secure digital payment systems.

Start AI forecasting with available market and sales data and improve accuracy as more transactions are collected.

Implementation Plan

Phase 1: Develop registration, authentication and product listing modules.

Phase 2: Implement consumer marketplace, bulk purchasing and order management.

Phase 3: Integrate AI-based demand forecasting.

Phase 4: Develop logistics management and route optimization.

Phase 5: Testing, security validation and pilot deployment.
